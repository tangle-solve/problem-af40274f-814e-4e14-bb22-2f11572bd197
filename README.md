### ALGO-000192: Asymptotically good quantum locally testable codes
- **Subfield:** Quantum complexity and locally decodable codes
- **Open:** Construct quantum codes simultaneously having constant rate, linear distance, constant-size checks, and constant soundness under a standard quantum local-testability definition.
- **Status:** Good quantum LDPC codes are known; adding constant local-testability soundness remains a stronger unresolved goal.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Would connect robust quantum error correction, locally checkable entanglement, and Hamiltonian complexity.
- **Key works:** Aharonov and Eldar (2015), *Quantum Locally Testable Codes*; quantum PCP workshop materials.
- **Related:** Quantum information—fault-tolerant memory; condensed matter—robust low-energy structure.
